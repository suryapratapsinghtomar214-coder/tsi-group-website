01 / ABOUT TSI GROUP

A group built around ideas, effort, and long-term vision.

TSI Group (Tomar Surya Industries) began on 1 July 2025 as a long-term vision to develop and organize projects across different industries.

The group concept includes business and creative divisions, from construction and building materials to technology, retail, books, and entertainment. Some divisions and projects are currently concepts or in development; this website presents the planned structure and can be updated as work becomes active.
